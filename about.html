About Willow House

Five brands, one bat-making standard.

Willow House started as a single workshop pressing bats for local club players. Today it runs five house brands, each with its own character, but every blade still passes through the same hands, the same balance checks, and the same standard of finish.

5 House Brands

4,000+ Bats Delivered
Our Purpose

Why we do this.

Most players either overpay for a name on the splice or settle for a bat that was never balanced for them. Willow House exists to close that gap: proper willow, honestly graded, priced for what it actually is, whether that's a collector-grade blade for an opening bat or a first bat for a ten-year-old.

Timeline

How the five brands came together.

2014

SS Thunder Blade Harrow

Willow House opens as Harrow, pressing English willow bats for club and school cricketers around Colombo.

2017

Gray-Nicolls Storm Elite Harrow

A second line built around thicker edges and a lower swell, aimed squarely at limited-overs batting.

2019

Kookaburra Shadow Force Harrow

A lighter, mid-swell shape for players who prioritise timing and bat speed over raw weight.

2021

MRF Power Crown Harrow

Our most restrained finish: unbranded stickers, hand-selected clefts, made in small batches.

2023

DSC Titan Drive Harrow

A value-focused line so a proper bat isn't out of reach for junior and developing players.
